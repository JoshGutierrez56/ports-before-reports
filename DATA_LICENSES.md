# Data licenses and permitted use

Code licensing does not grant source-dataset rights. Source snapshots are local, private-by-default reproducibility evidence. Do not publish raw vendor AIS, copied source websites/PDFs, or unnecessary vessel identifiers.

## IMF PortWatch

Attribution: **Sources: Kpler; UN Global Platform; IMF PortWatch**, https://portwatch.imf.org/ . [IMF copyright and usage terms](https://www.imf.org/en/about/copyright-and-terms) are linked by the official catalogs and HDX; this is **not CC0**. Statistical-data provisions permit attributed reuse and transformed analysis, with accurate representation and no endorsement implication. General terms also restrict automated bulk downloading and commercial reuse. The [PortWatch FAQ](https://portwatch.imf.org/pages/faqs) specifically documents programmatic API extraction and iteration by port IDs; its saved public page JSON provides the data-specific basis for this bounded three-gateway extraction. No general unrestricted scraping permission is claimed.

Local research uses the acquired aggregates. Reviewed site outputs may contain limited attributed transformed aggregates and original analysis, with current-vintage/methodology caveats, dataset links and these terms. Do not publish the full source panel, commercial redistribution, or branding that implies endorsement. PortWatch aggregate access does not license its underlying raw proprietary AIS. HDX is the same lineage, not an independent measurement or a more permissive license.

## BTS official targets

[Dataset y5ut-ibwt metadata](https://data.bts.gov/api/views/y5ut-ibwt.json) explicitly declares **Public Domain U.S. Government**, linking [government-works guidance](https://www.usa.gov/government-works). Selected rows identify BTS analysis of TEU data published by US ports, in TEUs and loaded-import status. Limited attributed target aggregates and original analysis may enter the public reproduction bundle. Other indicators in this mixed dataset have third-party sources; do not automatically extend the catalog license to all upstream proprietary inputs.

## Port-authority cross-checks

LA HTML, NYNJ trade-statistics PDF and GPA Savannah PDF verify facts, calendar scope and headers. Website/PDF text, graphics and design were not relicensed. Retain local evidence and source links rather than public copies or screenshots. Published numerical analysis uses the primary BTS vintage consistently, with the small Savannah differences disclosed. Long Beach's 403 response was not bypassed.
